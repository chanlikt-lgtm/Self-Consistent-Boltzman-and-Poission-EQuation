\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This document derives, from first principles, the deterministic spherical-harmonic (SHE)
Boltzmann solver of Liang \emph{et al.}, \emph{IEEE Trans.\ Electron Devices} \textbf{44}(2),
257--267 (1997), and reports a documented, benchmark-checked solver \emph{foundation} toward
reproducing the paper's Figs.~2--8. \textbf{Scope discipline:} this is not yet a validated
reproduction of Liang \emph{et al.}; the SHE core, production scattering, impact ionization,
the self-consistent loop, and the figure set are Part~II. Revision~3 responds to a second
audit of Rev.~2, resolving both items it flagged as blocking: (i) a real solver bug in the
zero-field electron temperature, now fixed by adding the omitted acoustic-phonon energy
operator (\S\ref{sec:bulk}); and (ii) the global total-energy ($H$) grid definition
(\S\ref{sec:she2}, App.~\ref{sec:specs}). It also corrects the impact-ionization citation,
restructures the benchmark table into genuine benchmarks vs.\ construction targets vs.\
sanity trends, gives an \emph{extracted} threshold voltage, and adds code provenance.
Every substitution for unavailable paper inputs is disclosed and classified
(\S\ref{sec:taxonomy}).
\end{abstract}

\section{Revision history and audit responses}\label{sec:audit}
\textbf{Rev.\ 1$\to$2} fixed eight findings (claims, specs, references, junction geometry,
$N_c$ convergence, impact-ionization bookkeeping). \textbf{Rev.\ 2$\to$3} addresses the
second audit, whose two ``stop Part~II'' items were genuine implementation/specification
errors:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
\item[\textbf{B1}] \textbf{Zero-field $T_e=282$~K did not match the report's own moment
definition (a real bug).} Root cause: the optical-phonon operator connects only energies
separated by $E_{op}=5\Delta\eps$, so the energy grid split into five decoupled
sub-lattices; with no field the operator was reducible and the equilibrium was
undetermined. \emph{Fix:} added the physically-omitted acoustic-phonon energy operator
(a Scharfetter--Gummel Fokker--Planck term coupling adjacent energies, with the Maxwellian
as its exact discrete null). The bulk solver now reproduces the Maxwellian to
$3\times10^{-4}$ and gives $T_e(0)=311$~K, converging to the analytic nonparabolic value
$309.7$~K as $\Delta\eps\to0$ (\S\ref{sec:bulk}).
\item[\textbf{B2}] \textbf{Global $H$-grid inconsistency.} Rev.~2 wrote the ``$H$ grid'' as
$[0,3.02]$~eV, which is the \emph{kinetic-energy} range and is wrong for $H=\eps-q\phi$:
at the $+0.586$~V contact a thermal electron has $H\approx-0.586$~eV. \emph{Fix:} the
global $H$-mesh is defined as $-q\phi_{\max}\le H\le \eps_{\max}-q\phi_{\min}$ with the
physical mask $0\le H+q\phi\le\eps_{\max}$ (\S\ref{sec:she2}, App.~\ref{sec:specs}).
\end{itemize}
Remaining second-audit findings: impact-ionization citation corrected and equal-split
relabelled a disclosed assumption (\S\ref{sec:ii}); benchmark table split into three classes
(\S\ref{sec:bench}); band model (Kane surrogate vs.\ paper's Fiegna/Brunetti) disambiguated
(\S\ref{sec:taxonomy}); threshold voltage now \emph{extracted}, not inferred, with the
calibration degeneracy resolved (\S\ref{sec:bench}); layout overflow of the reconstruction
notes fixed; code commit + dependency versions added (title, App.~\ref{sec:provenance});
and ``bulk-prototype validated'' distinguished from ``2-D production scattering pending''.

\section{Derivation of the first-order SHE equation}\label{sec:derivation}
With $\vecE=-\gradr\phi$ and electron force $-q\vecE$, expand $f=f_0(\vecr,\eps)+\Om\cdot
\mathbf{f}_1(\vecr,\eps)$ (isotropic part plus a single drift term; all $\ell\ge2$ content
discarded --- an \textbf{energy-resolved distribution with first-order angular anisotropy}).
Projection gives the current relation and the conservative augmented-space balance,
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{f}_1=-\tau_1 v\big(\gradr f_0-q\vecE\,\partial_\eps f_0\big),\qquad
\gradr\!\cdot\vecj_x+\partial_\eps j_\eps= Z\,\mathcal{Q}[f_0]+Z\,S_{\text{gen}},
\label{eq:she}
\end{equation}
with $\vecj_x=-ZD(\gradr f_0-q\vecE\,\partial_\eps f_0)$, $j_\eps=-q\vecE\!\cdot\vecj_x$,
$D=\tfrac13 v^2\tau_1$.

\subsection{Total-energy form and the global $H$-grid}\label{sec:she2}
With $H=\eps-q\phi$ ($\eps=H+q\phi\ge0$), \eqref{eq:she} reduces to pure spatial diffusion
at fixed $H$,
\begin{equation}
\gradr\!\cdot\!\big[ZD\,\gradr f_0|_H\big]=-Z\mathcal{Q}[f_0]-ZS_{\text{gen}},\qquad
\eps=H+q\phi(\vecr),
\label{eq:she_H}
\end{equation}
solved entirely in $(\vecr,H)$: per-$H$-plane spatial diffusion; the collision operator
couples planes $H\!\leftrightarrow\!H\pm E_{op}$ at the same $(x,y)$; no energy--space
cross-flux. \fix{B2} The global mesh spans
\begin{equation}
-q\phi_{\max}\;\le\;H\;\le\;\eps_{\max}-q\phi_{\min},\qquad
\text{physical mask}\;\; 0\le H+q\phi(\vecr)\le\eps_{\max},
\end{equation}
so that a thermal ($\eps\approx0$) electron at any contact potential is represented; nodes
failing the mask are classically forbidden and carry a zero-flux turning-point boundary.

\subsection{Scattering operators}
Elastic momentum randomization (acoustic, ionized-impurity Brooks--Herring screened by
$n_{scr}$, surface terms) enters $1/\tau_1$ and $D$. The inelastic optical operator
$\mathcal{Q}_{op}$ is the number-conserving in/out form (App.~\ref{sec:specs}), vanishing
for a Maxwellian (detailed balance, with $Z(\eps<0)\equiv0$). \fix{B1} Because $\mathcal{Q}
_{op}$ only connects $\eps\leftrightarrow\eps\pm E_{op}$, an \emph{acoustic-phonon energy}
operator is required to couple adjacent energies and make the collision operator
irreducible; we use the conservative Fokker--Planck form
$-\partial_\eps[ZA(\partial_\eps+1/k_BT)f_0]$, $A(\eps)=4m^*v_s^2\eps/\tau_{ac}$,
Scharfetter--Gummel--discretized so the discrete Maxwellian is its exact null.

\subsection{Impact ionization}\label{sec:ii}
An event removes one primary electron above $\eps_{th}$ and creates one electron--hole pair;
the two resulting electrons share the post-event kinetic energy. The $\ell=0$ operator loses
the primary at $\eps$ and re-injects two carriers near $(\eps-\eps_{th})/2$, giving net
electron gain $=\int Z f_0/\tau_{ii}\,d\eps\equiv G_n=G_{p,\text{ii}}$ (charge conserved,
paper Eq.~13). \fix{second audit} The equal energy split is a \textbf{disclosed modeling
assumption} (not a specific result of Landsberg~[9], whose 1972 paper concerns impact
ionization and Auger recombination in bands); Part~II may replace it with the secondary
distribution of ref.~[6].
\honest{The rate $1/\tau_{ii}$ from ref.~[6] (Wu \& Goldsman, random-$k$) is unavailable;
Part~II uses a Keldysh form $1/\tau_{ii}=P[(\eps-\eps_{th})/\eps_{th}]^2\Theta(\eps-\eps_{th})$,
$\eps_{th}\!\approx\!\SI{1.1}{eV}$, calibrated to the measured Si $\alpha_n(E)$ --- a
calibrated surrogate (\S\ref{sec:taxonomy}). $P$ will be reported once calibrated.}

Smooth error-function junctions; $x_j$ defined as the metallurgical crossing and retuned to
$\SI{0.170}{\micro\meter}$. \fix{second audit, band clarification} The paper uses a

\paragraph{Threshold voltage --- extracted, degeneracy resolved \fix{F3}.}
The two electrostatic offsets are the channel-implant peak $N_A$ and the gate offset
$\Phi_{\text{gate}}$. To avoid the non-unique two-knobs/one-target fit the audit flagged,
\emph{both are held at fixed inputs} ($N_A=\SI{4e17}{cm^{-3}}$, $\Phi_{\text{gate}}=
\SI{0.30}{V}$) and $V_{th}$ is a \emph{derived} quantity, extracted by max-transconductance
linear extrapolation ($V_{ds}=\SI{0.1}{V}$): $\boxed{V_{th}=\SI{1.64}{V}}$. This is
\emph{too high}: the $\SI{4e17}{}$ implant, set in Rev.~1 ``for a realistic $V_{th}$'' but
never verified, over-dopes the channel. Measured sensitivity is a rigid shift
$dV_{th}/d\Phi_{\text{gate}}\approx1$. \textbf{Action for Part~II:} lower $N_A$ to
$\sim\!\SI{1.2e17}{cm^{-3}}$ to reach a paper-consistent $V_{th}\approx\SI{0.6}{V}$, then
regenerate the operating point.

\paragraph{Drift-diffusion current, with the correct overdrive.} Using the \emph{extracted}
$V_{th}=\SI{1.64}{V}$, the overdrive at $V_g=\SI{3}{V}$ is $\SI{1.36}{V}$ and the
long-channel estimate $\tfrac1L\mu_{\text{inv}}C_{ox}(V_g-V_{th})^2/2=\SI{0.28}{mA/\micro
\meter}$, versus DD $\SI{0.25}{mA/\micro\meter}$ --- agreement to $\sim\!14\%$. (Rev.~2's
apparent $3.5\times$ discrepancy was an artifact of assuming $V_{th}=\SI{0.5}{V}$; the
extracted value removes it, leaving only the expected small velocity-saturation reduction.)

\section{Numerical and model specifications}\label{sec:specs}
\textbf{Constants:} $T=\SI{300}{K}$, $V_t=\SI{25.85}{mV}$, $n_i=\SI{1.45e10}{cm^{-3}}$,
Boltzmann statistics, $\epsilon_{si}=11.7$, $\epsilon_{ox}=3.9$, $m^*=0.32m_0$, $g_v=6$,
$\alpha=\SI{0.5}{eV^{-1}}$, $v_s=\SI{9.04e3}{m/s}$.
\textbf{Doping (erfc profiles), lengths in \si{\micro\meter}:} donors
$N_D=10^{20}[L(x;0.15)D(y;0.105)+R(x;0.65)D(y;0.105)]+10^{18}[L(x;0.22)D(y;0.06)+R(x;0.58)
D(y;0.06)]$ where $L(x;e)=\tfrac12\mathrm{erfc}((x-e)/0.028)$, $R(x;e)=\tfrac12\mathrm{erfc}
((e-x)/0.028)$, $D(y;x_j)=\tfrac12\mathrm{erfc}((y-x_j)/0.025)$; acceptors $N_A=10^{16}+
4\times10^{17}\cdot\tfrac12[\mathrm{erfc}((x-0.62)/0.028)-\mathrm{erfc}((x-0.18)/0.028)]
D(y;0.06)$. Gate $x\in[0.20,0.60]$; source contact $x\le0.15$, drain $x\ge0.65$ (top);
body = bottom row.
\textbf{Mesh:} tensor grid, surface-refined $y\propto s^{1.8}$; figures use
$N_x{\times}N_y=90{\times}80$ (Poisson/bands), $80{\times}70$ (DD bias), $56{\times}48$
($V_{th}$ sweep). $\eps$-grid uniform $\Delta\eps=\SI{2.5}{meV}$ (bulk); global $H$-grid per
\S\ref{sec:she2}. \textbf{Poisson BCs:} ohmic Dirichlet $\phi=V+V_t\,\mathrm{asinh}(N_{net}/
2n_i)$; \emph{gate oxide Robin BC}: transmissibility $T_{ox}=\epsilon_{ox}\epsilon_0\Delta
x_i/t_{ox}$ ($t_{ox}=\SI{9.6}{nm}$) to a metal node at $V_g-\Phi_{\text{gate}}$,
$\Phi_{\text{gate}}=\SI{0.30}{V}$; Neumann elsewhere. Newton clip \SI{0.5}{V}, tol
\SI{e-8}{V}. \textbf{Continuity:} SG current $J_{n,a\to b}=\tfrac{q\mu_nV_t}{h}[B(\Delta)n_b
-B(-\Delta)n_a]$, $B(x)=x/(e^x-1)$; ohmic $n=\tfrac12(N_{net}+\sqrt{N_{net}^2+4n_i^2})$,
$p=n_i^2/n$. \textbf{Mobility:} Caughey--Thomas (electrons $\mu_{\min,\max}/N_{ref}/a=
68.5/1414/9.2{\times}10^{16}/0.711$; holes $44.9/470.5/2.23{\times}10^{17}/0.719$), field
saturation $v_{sat}^n=\SI{1.07e7}{cm/s},\beta_n{=}2$; $v_{sat}^p=\SI{8.37e6}{cm/s},
\beta_p{=}1$. \textbf{SRH:} $\tau_n=\tau_p=\SI{e-7}{s}$, semi-implicit. \textbf{Gummel} tol
\SI{e-5}{V}. \textbf{Optical operator:} $Z\mathcal{Q}_{op}=c_{op}Z[(N{+}1)Z_{+}f_{+}+
NZ_{-}f_{-}-((N{+}1)Z_{-}+NZ_{+})f]$, $c_{op}=\SI{4.69e-34}{J\,m^3/s}$ (from
$\tau_{op}^{ref}=\SI{e-13}{s}$), $\tau_1=\SI{e-14}{s}/(\sqrt{\gamma}\gamma')$.
\textbf{Contact current:} drain electron current $=\sum$ SG fluxes into drain nodes,
per \si{\micro\meter} ($\times10^{-4}$ from A/cm). \textbf{SHE contacts (Part~II):}
$f_0=$ normalized Maxwellian $Z^{-1}n_{contact}\,e^{-\eps/k_BT}/\!\int Z e^{-\eps/k_BT}d\eps$.
