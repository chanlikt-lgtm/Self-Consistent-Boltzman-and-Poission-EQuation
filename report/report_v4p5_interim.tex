\begin{abstract}
Revision~5 established that Anderson acceleration converges the coupled
SHE$\leftrightarrow$Poisson$\leftrightarrow$hole fixed point on each fixed mesh, but that the
tail-sensitive impact-ionization rate $\Gii$ remained \emph{spatially} mesh-dependent. This interim
note documents the controlled spatial-convergence campaign that followed, now complete. Five results
are firm: (i) the earlier uniform $40\times34\to60\times50\to80\times66$ sequence was itself
\emph{under-resolving} the drain avalanche, so its values were inflated; (ii) a single further
drain-refined mesh cannot settle the question because refining resolution and changing the drain
grading law are confounded; (iii) a properly controlled self-similar trio
$71\times58\to100\times70\to141\times85$ --- fixed grading law, byte-identical frozen energy grid
(SHA1-verified), $\sqrt2$ interval refinement --- is the first sequence capable of answering grid
stability cleanly; (iv) the nominal 60-outer-iteration budget \emph{materially} truncated the
nonlinear settling on the finer meshes, so node values must be accepted on an observable-plateau
gate, not an iteration count; and (v) with all three nodes equalized to $\sim\!10^{-4}$ relative
plateau quality, the controlled trio is \textbf{genuinely non-monotone}
($2.990\to2.135\to2.413\times10^{25}$~cm$^{-3}$s$^{-1}$): the down-then-up reversal exceeds the
combined plateau band by a factor $\sim\!870$, so \emph{no} Richardson continuum extrapolation is
admissible. The drain-localized $\Gii$ is therefore \emph{not} in an asymptotic spatial-convergence
regime along this refinement path; the model gap to Liang's top contour remains order $10^{2}$ and is
stated only as a not-converged tested-grid quantity.
\end{abstract}

\status{\textbf{Resolved.} All three controlled nodes are converged to comparable
($\sim\!10^{-4}$) plateau quality. The trio is \textbf{genuinely non-monotone}; the reversal is
numerically unambiguous ($S_{\mathrm{rev}}\approx870$), so \textbf{Richardson extrapolation is
suppressed} and no continuum $\Gii$ is quoted from this trio.}

\section{Scope and the three-way convergence distinction}
The quantity under study is the peak impact-ionization generation rate $\Gii$ (and its
cross-sectional integral $\Gtot=\int G_{ii}\,dA$) at the drain of the $0.35\,\mu$m LDD NMOS at
$V_{gs}=V_{ds}=3$~V, $V_{th}=0.614$~V. Three distinct notions of ``convergence'' must be kept
separate:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Coupling (fixed-point) convergence} --- does the nonlinear outer loop reach its
  fixed point on a \emph{given} mesh? \emph{Resolved} in Rev.~5 by Anderson acceleration.
  \item \textbf{Spatial (mesh) convergence} --- does the plateaued node value stop moving as the
  real-space mesh is refined? \emph{The subject of this note.}
  \item \textbf{Energy-grid sensitivity} --- dependence on $\Delta H$, $\epsilon_{\max}$, and the
  frozen $H$-domain. Small ($\sim2.4\%$ one-way $\Delta H$ in Rev.~5); here energy-grid variation is
  \emph{removed as a between-mesh confounder} by construction (one common frozen $H$-grid across all
  meshes) --- which does not eliminate energy-discretization error itself, only its variation across
  the trio.
\end{itemize}

$\Gii$ and $\Gtot$ fall by the \emph{same} factors ($-73\%$ then $-30\%$), so the mesh dependence is
in the total avalanche magnitude, not merely peak-cell placement: coarse grids over-populate the
high-energy tail at the under-resolved drain hotspot. A drain-graded $100\times70$ mesh
(drain $dx=0.0027\,\mu$m, $\sim\!3.8\times$ finer at the drain) plateaus at $\Gii=1.80\times10^{25}$
--- roughly an order of magnitude below the ``finest uniform'' $80\times66$ --- confirming the
uniform values were inflated by drain under-resolution.

\section{Why one further mesh is not enough: a confounded fifth point}
A second drain-refined mesh ($140\times84$, drain $dx=0.00126\,\mu$m) plateaus cleanly at
$\Gii=2.72\times10^{25}$ --- \emph{above} the $100\times70$ value. Taken at face value this looks
non-monotone, but that mesh changed \emph{two} things at once: it refined resolution \emph{and}
altered the drain grading (ratio $6\!\to\!10$, width $W=0.09\!\to\!0.06\,\mu$m). The $+51\%$ change
cannot be attributed to resolution alone.
\honest{This is exactly why a heterogeneous refinement sequence cannot establish spatial
convergence: resolution and node-distribution effects are entangled. Richardson extrapolation on such
a sequence would be meaningless. The remedy is a \emph{self-similar} family in which only $N_x,N_y$
change.}

We fix the grading law (ratio $=6$, $W=0.09\,\mu$m, $X_C=0.60\,\mu$m; $y=L_y s^{1.8}$) and refine
only resolution, choosing node counts so that the \emph{measured} drain $dx$ and surface $dy$ both
refine by $\approx\sqrt2$:

\paragraph{Comparability guards (enforced by the driver, fail-fast).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{One common frozen energy grid.} A single imposed span (COMMON\_PHI\_SPAN $=[-0.60,
  3.65]$~V, bounding the measured $\phi\in[-0.348,3.586]$~V) fixes the $H$-grid so it is
  \emph{byte-identical} across all meshes; the realized $H$-array is SHA1-hashed
  (\texttt{b4319bb03303} on all nodes) and the extrapolation script refuses to run if the hashes
  differ. A converged $\phi$ leaving the span is a hard abort. This removes any smuggled
  energy-discretization change from a spatial-only study.
  \item \textbf{Identical solver settings} --- $\Delta H=12.5$~meV, $\epsilon_{\max}=3.02$~eV,
  \texttt{she\_tol}$=10^{-8}$, Anderson depth 6, $\beta=0.5$, step-cap 5, restart 6, same stopping
  rule and last-4 plateau definition --- printed as a run manifest before every solve.
  \item \textbf{Measured spacings recorded} (drain $dx$, surface $dy$, and $dy$ at the avalanche
  depth), with a warning that the depth spacing refines at $\approx1.2$, \emph{not} $\sqrt2$: the
  sequence is surface-isotropic (appropriate for the surface-localized $\Gii$) but not globally
  isotropic (relevant for the depth-sampling $\Gtot$).
\end{itemize}

\section{Methodological finding: iteration budget vs.\ plateau}
The finest node exposed a systematic issue. At the nominal 60-iteration cap, $141\times85$ read
$\Gii\approx2.20\times10^{25}$ with a still-drifting observable; continued under \emph{identical}
settings it climbed and locked at $2.413\times10^{25}$ (last-4 spread $6\times10^{-5}$). Likewise
$71\times58$ moved from $3.069$ to $2.990\times10^{25}$ on continuation.
\honest{Equal outer-iteration counts do \emph{not} imply equal fixed-point convergence quality
across meshes: the finer mesh needs substantially more nonlinear iterations to settle. Node values
must therefore be accepted on an \emph{observable-plateau} gate --- $R_\phi$ at its floor
\emph{and} last-4 spreads at the $O(10^{-4})$ level ($\lesssim3\times10^{-4}$) in both $\Gii$ and
$\Gtot$, applied identically to all three nodes --- not on a fixed iteration budget. Operationally,
``$R_\phi$ at its floor'' means the true fixed-point residual $\max|G(\phi)-\phi|$ has stopped
descending and merely oscillates within a noise band set by the inner solve
(\texttt{she\_tol}$=10^{-8}$): here $R_\phi\lesssim10^{-3}$~V with no monotone decrease over the last
several iterations, rather than a value still trending downward. (The final $R_\phi$ never reaches the
nominal $10^{-4}$~V \texttt{tol\_phi}; that tolerance is not the operative gate --- the observable
plateau is.) This is itself a
reportable numerical lesson, and it is why the coarser nodes are re-flattened before any differencing.}

\section{Analysis framework and applied verdict}
The evaluation order was fixed \emph{before} $G_2$ converged, and was not changed by the outcome:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Monotonicity with numerical significance.} With signed corrections
  $\Delta_{12}=G_1-G_2$, $\Delta_{23}=G_2-G_3$ and per-node plateau bands $u_i$ (last-4 half-range),
  a sign reversal is judged by the \emph{heuristic} ratio
  \[
    S_{\mathrm{rev}}=\frac{|\Delta_{\mathrm{reversal}}|}{\sqrt{u_i^2+u_j^2}}
    \qquad(\text{reversal-to-plateau-uncertainty ratio, \emph{not} a statistical }\sigma).
  \]
  $S_{\mathrm{rev}}\gg1$: the reversal far exceeds residual fixed-point drift $\Rightarrow$ genuinely
  non-monotone $\Rightarrow$ suppress extrapolation. $S_{\mathrm{rev}}\lesssim O(1)$: unresolved by
  plateau precision $\Rightarrow$ read as grid stability near the finest nodes.
  \item \textbf{Contraction (only if monotone).} Require $|\Delta_{23}|<|\Delta_{12}|$.
  \item \textbf{Richardson (only if contracting).} With near-constant ratio $r\approx\sqrt2$,
  \[
    p=\frac{\ln|\Delta_{12}/\Delta_{23}|}{\ln r},\qquad
    G_\infty=G_3+\frac{G_3-G_2}{r^{\,p}-1},
  \]
  computed by an authoritative unequal-ratio 3-point solve on the full-precision measured spacings
  (drain-$dx$ ratios $1.41436,\,1.41437$, i.e.\ $\sqrt2$ to $4\times10^{-4}$) with the
  $\sqrt2$ closed form as a transparency cross-check. Because only three controlled meshes exist,
  $G_\infty$ is reported \emph{as a bundle} with $p$, the correction ratio $|\Delta_{23}|/|\Delta_{12}|$,
  and the two-$h$ agreement --- as evidence of asymptotic behaviour, not proof of the continuum value.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Applied verdict.} The converged trio is
\[
  G_1=2.990\times10^{25},\quad G_2=2.135\times10^{25},\quad G_3=2.413\times10^{25}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}s^{-1}},
\]
with signed corrections $\Delta_{12}=+8.55\times10^{24}$ (decrease) and $\Delta_{23}=-2.78\times10^{24}$
(increase): \textbf{down then up}. The middle node lies below \emph{both} neighbours, so the sequence
is non-monotone. The reversal magnitude relative to the plateau band is
\[
  S_{\mathrm{rev}}=\frac{|\Delta_{23}|}{\sqrt{u_2^2+u_3^2}}
  =\frac{2.78\times10^{24}}{3.2\times10^{21}}\approx874\gg1,
\]
i.e.\ the upturn exceeds the residual fixed-point drift by nearly three orders of magnitude. The
integral $\Gtot$ behaves identically: with $G_2^{\Gtot}=6.027\times10^{13}$,
$G_3^{\Gtot}=6.593\times10^{13}$ ($|\Delta_{23}^{\Gtot}|=5.65\times10^{12}$) and combined plateau band
$6.84\times10^{9}$ (from the $\Gtot$ half-ranges in \S6),
$S_{\mathrm{rev}}^{\Gtot}=5.65\times10^{12}/6.84\times10^{9}\approx826$. All comparability gates pass:
mesh-law/solver signatures identical, frozen $H$-array SHA1 \texttt{b4319bb03303} identical on all
three, and (full-precision measured spacings) $r_x=1.41436,\,1.41437$,
$r_{y,\mathrm{surf}}=1.410,\,1.425$.
\honest{The controlled trio is \emph{genuinely non-monotone}, not merely unconverged. Because the
non-monotonicity survived equalizing all three fixed points to $\sim\!10^{-4}$ quality, it cannot be
dismissed as unequal convergence quality. This sign-changing trio does not support the assumed
leading-order single-power Richardson model, so \textbf{extrapolation is suppressed}: no continuum
$\Gii$ is inferred from this trio. This is the defensible ceiling of the spatial study along this refinement path.}

\section{Model gap (a not-converged tested-grid statement)}
Across the controlled trio the converged nodes span
$\Gii=2.135$--$2.990\times10^{25}$~cm$^{-3}$s$^{-1}$; the finest mesh gives $2.413\times10^{25}$.
Relative to Liang's top published $G_{ii}$ contour ($2.3\times10^{27}$~cm$^{-3}$s$^{-1}$) this is a
factor $77$--$108$, i.e.\ order $10^{2}$, below.
\honest{Because the controlled sequence is non-monotone, no converged spatial value and no continuum
extrapolation can be \emph{inferred from this trio} to anchor a final model gap. The order-$10^{2}$
figure is a \emph{tested-grid} statement over the drain refinements performed, not a continuum/model
gap. The remaining discrepancy is \emph{consistent with a substantial model/physics discrepancy after
the tested spatial refinements} (surrogate Keldysh impact-ionization rate on reconstructed doping,
as in Rev.~5), but it cannot yet be called definitively physics-dominated because the spatial error
is not bounded by a continuum study. What the present study does show firmly is that the
\emph{numerical} spatial contribution is larger and more subtle than a smooth monotone convergence
would imply.}

\section{Conclusions and outlook}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Coupling convergence:} resolved (Anderson, per mesh).
  \item \textbf{Spatial convergence: not demonstrated.} The controlled three-grid sequence is
  \emph{significantly} non-monotone ($2.990\to2.135\to2.413\times10^{25}$, $S_{\mathrm{rev}}\approx874$),
  so no continuum value can be inferred from this refinement trio. This does \emph{not} establish that
  a continuum limit fails to exist --- finer self-similar meshes could still enter a monotone
  asymptotic regime; it establishes that this trio has not entered one. The non-monotonicity itself is
  a firm, numerically-resolved result.
  \item \textbf{Energy grid:} energy-grid \emph{variation} removed as a between-mesh confounder (one
  common frozen $H$-grid, SHA1-identical) --- not a claim that energy-discretization error is zero.
  \item \textbf{Model gap:} order $10^{2}$ below Liang's top contour ($77$--$108\times$), stated as a
  not-converged tested-grid quantity; consistent with a substantial model/physics discrepancy after
  the tested refinements, but not yet definitively physics-dominated (spatial error still unbounded).
\end{itemize}
\paragraph{What would be needed to go further.} Because $\Gii$ is a tail-sensitive moment at a
near-singular drain hotspot, the natural next instruments are (a) a \emph{second} controlled family
--- fixed $y$-mesh with systematic drain-$x$ refinement --- to separate $x$- and $y$-discretization
error and test whether non-monotonicity is direction-specific; and (b) a mesh law with finite surface
slope so all local $dy$ refine linearly, enabling a genuinely isotropic global study. Neither is
required to state the present conclusion; both would sharpen \emph{why} the drain avalanche resists
monotone spatial convergence. Physics-model changes (surrogate impact-ionization rate, reconstructed
doping) remain the dominant lever on the absolute gap to Liang, and are deferred by design until the
numerical characterization is complete.

\honest{Methodological lesson to carry forward: a fixed outer-iteration budget does not guarantee
comparable fixed-point quality across meshes. Here the coarser nodes had to be continued well beyond
60 iterations, and their converged values shifted by up to $+18\%$; the non-monotonicity verdict is
only trustworthy \emph{because} it was evaluated on equalized, plateau-gated fixed points with an
explicit reversal-significance test ($S_{\mathrm{rev}}$), not on truncated iterates.}
