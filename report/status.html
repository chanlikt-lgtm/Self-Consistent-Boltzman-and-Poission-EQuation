External audit → response

An independent audit graded Revision 1 “promising Part I; not reproduction-grade yet.” All eight findings are addressed at the source in Revision 2.

F1

“Full momentum-space distribution” overclaims a first-order truncation.

→ Reworded to energy-resolved distribution with first-order angular anisotropy; all ℓ ≥ 2 content is explicitly discarded.

F2

Simulation not reproducible — boundary conditions, statistics, mobility, mesh, tolerances, sign convention all missing.

→ New specifications appendix documents every BC (incl. the gate oxide Robin BC), Caughey–Thomas mobility + parameters, SRH, mesh, Newton/Gummel tolerances, and the contact-current sign.

F3

Junction geometry mismatch: plot shows ≈0.24 µm, text says xj≈0.17 µm.

→ Defined xj as the metallurgical crossing and retuned the profile to 0.170 µm (verified). Vth calibration + sensitivity documented.

F4

H-transform vs. discretization inconsistent — sounds like (r,ε) and (r,H) forms were mixed.

→ The device solver is now stated entirely in (r,H): pure spatial diffusion per H-plane, scattering couples H-planes at ±Eop, no energy-space cross-flux. SG stabilizes only the spatial flux.

F5

Nc “0.5% match” not auditable: T never stated; auditor gets 2.86×1019 at 300 K.

→ Real fix. Rev-1’s 2.785×1019 was under-converged (400-pt grid). Converged (4000-pt) value is 2.857×1019 at stated 300 K — matches the auditor to 0.1%; parabolic limit vs. closed form agrees to 0.09% (prefactor proven).

F6

Impact-ionization electron bookkeeping incomplete — only the hole integral was given.

→ Derived the electron operator: primary lost above εth, two carriers re-injected at (ε−εth)/2, net Δn = Δp = Gii (charge conserved).

F7

“Validated” claims are sanity checks; DD current vs. an undocumented estimate.

→ Relabelled sanity-checked with a quantitative benchmark table. DD current honesty: long-channel 0.96 vs. DD 0.25 mA/µm — the gap is velocity saturation, not “matching.”

F8

No references; dangling [23],[25]; no code/version provenance.

→ Added a 13-entry references section and a code + parameter provenance appendix.
